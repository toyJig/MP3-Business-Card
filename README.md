This is one of the projects I've taken on to learn electronics and PCB design more in-depth. I had a career fair coming up, and I've always seen those guys on the internet with sick interactive custom-PCB business cards. So I thought, I want one too! Little did I know, this prroject wouldn't turn out as I had hoped with the battery constraints, but It's still something I'm relatiively proud of and can (hopefully) give out to recruiters- but only those I'm actually interested in since these are going to cost like 10$ each. If you got one from me, congrats? Hope it doesn't explode in your pocket haha

I really wanted this project to become a business card with what I imagined to just be a coin battery for the source. However, I soon found out that the max current draw on coin batteries is little to none compared to even running a single rp2040. The LiPo coin batteries looked promising but they're sort of out of my budget. So... LiPo pouch it is.
